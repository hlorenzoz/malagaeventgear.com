# Ecosystem carousel in Google Search


To provide additional, relevant sources of information, the ecosystem carousel may appear when
people search for specific information that benefit from data visualizations or interactivity
with data (for example, stock charts, weather information, sports scores, translation).
![Ecosystem carousel in Google Search](https://developers.google.com/static/search/docs/images/ecosystem-carousel.png)


After clicking a tile in the carousel, the searcher is taken directly to that website. The order
of websites in the carousel is determined by Search ranking algorithms based on non-discriminatory
ranking principles designed to ensure users see the most relevant results for their location and
language. To be eligible to appear in the carousel, check to see if you
[meet the requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ecosystem-carousel#requirements), and if you do,
[express interest in the feature](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ecosystem-carousel#express-interest).

## Feature availability


This feature is available in
[European Economic Area](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Glossary:European_Economic_Area_(EEA))
(EEA) countries for queries related to weather, sports, finance, and translate on both desktop and
mobile devices.

## Requirements


To be eligible to appear in the ecosystem carousel, your website must meet the following
requirements:

- Your website must serve users in the EEA.
- Your website publishes relevant finance, weather, sports information, or provides translation.
- Your website must be an authoritative source or a specialized provider in your respective domains (for example, major media outlets, or commercial weather services).

> [!NOTE]
> **Note**: No new structured data markup or special feeds are required for this specific feature.

## Express interest


If your business serves users in the EEA, and you believe you are an eligible provider of
finance, weather, or sports information, or provide translation, you can express interest by
filling out our
[interest form](https://support.google.com/websearch/contact/ecosystem_carousel).
