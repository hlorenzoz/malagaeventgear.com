# Regional differences in Search experience


Google is evolving the search results page for certain types of queries to provide new
opportunities for Vertical Search Services (VSS), Comparison Shopping Services (CSS), direct
suppliers, and content providers.


To find the features relevant to your website, select where your company is based and the query
types that are most relevant to your business:
