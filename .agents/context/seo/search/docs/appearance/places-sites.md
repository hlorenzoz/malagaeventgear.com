# Places sites: aggregator carousel and refinement chip


This page describes features for Places sites in Google Search, including the aggregator
carousel and refinement chips for local business and hotel queries in Türkiye. Publishers don't
need to add any markup to be eligible for the aggregator carousel or refinement chips.
![Places sites aggregator carousel and refinement chip in Google Search](https://developers.google.com/static/search/docs/images/places-sites.png)


The "Places sites" aggregator carousel allows users to easily see the top aggregator results for
their respective query. Users can also click **More sites** to see additional relevant
aggregator sites for their query.


The "Places sites" refinement chip can help users focus the Search page on aggregator text
results. Searchers can activate the refinement chips at the top of the Search results page or by
clicking **More sites** on the aggregator carousel.

## Feature availability


These new experiences are available to users in Türkiye who are searching for queries related to
local businesses and hotels.
